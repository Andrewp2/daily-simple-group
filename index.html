Symmetry has atoms

A group is a collection of symmetries

A square has 8 symmetries: 4 rotations and 4 flips. Doing one after another always gives another symmetry, and that rule is what makes them a group. The number of symmetries, here 8, is the group's order.

Simple groups are the atoms

Most groups can be broken into smaller groups, the way 12 breaks into 2 × 2 × 3. The ones that cannot be broken are called simple. Every finite group is built out of simple groups.

The classification lists all of them

The Classification of Finite Simple Groups says every finite simple group belongs to one of 18 infinite families, or is one of 26 sporadic groups that fit no family. The proof took about 100 mathematicians five decades and tens of thousands of pages. Axiom has now checked the whole proof by machine, in Lean 4.
